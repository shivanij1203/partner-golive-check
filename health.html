Keep live partners working

Partners change their sites without telling anyone, and a redesign or a new cookie banner can quietly break offers. Your own data shows when something changes: offer views drop, or the order details the offer code sends start looking wrong. It doesn't show why. That signal starts a recheck of the partner right away, and the AI agent finds what changed on the page and writes the fix for their developer.

Try it: send a signal Each button breaks a random partner's page the way a real site update might, then sends the signal your data would show. The data is simulated here. Demo breaks undo themselves after half an hour.
Offer views drop Offers stop showing, so views fall to zero. The agent finds out why.
Order details look wrong Views look normal, but orders arrive without the shopper's permission, or with the same order number every time. The agent finds out why.

    In a real setup

    • Your own alerting would send the signal when a partner's views drop or their order details look wrong. The checker has a hook for it.
    • A regular recheck of every live partner is the backstop, for low-traffic partners and for a page that changed before any shopper reached it.
    • The partner's page would come from a saved test order rather than a public link, and the checks would follow Fluent's own spec.
