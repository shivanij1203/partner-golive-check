Keep live partners working

Partners change their sites without telling anyone. A redesign or a new cookie banner can quietly stop offers from showing, and the partner only notices when their earnings drop. The AI agent rechecks every live partner's page every few hours and tells the partner team the moment one breaks, with the fix.

Try it Pretend one of these partners just updated their site. The monitor rechecks that partner right away instead of waiting for the next round.
