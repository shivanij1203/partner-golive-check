About this project

What this is, why I built it, and how

What this is

A working concept of a tool for Fluent's partner team. An AI agent checks a partner's checkout page before offers go live, then keeps watching it after launch. Everything on this site runs for real, on example pages I built.

Why I built it

I applied for Fluent's Software Engineer (AI Agents) role. Most of Fluent's business now runs through commerce partners, and getting each one live and keeping it working is still largely done by hand. A mistake there quietly costs the partner and Fluent money, so it felt like the right problem to try the job on.

How I approached it

  1. Read public guides on how post-purchase offers are set up.
  2. Listed the mistakes that fail quietly.
  3. Built example pages with those mistakes.
  4. Let the AI agent read the page, but kept the final call in code.
  5. Tested it, including a page that tries to trick it.
  6. Added monitoring for after launch.

The problem

When a partner adds offers to their checkout, small setup mistakes are easy to miss. Most of them fail quietly, so nobody notices until money is lost.

After launch, it keeps watching

Partners update their sites all the time. The same AI agent rechecks every live partner every few hours and alerts the team the moment a change breaks offers, with the fix, before the partner notices lower earnings.

See partner health

Why it matters

Once offers are live, a partner earns on every order. Each day a setup problem delays launch, that money is missed by the partner and by Fluent.
